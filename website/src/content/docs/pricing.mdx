## How the trial works

- Sign up, set up your bar, and give your card details at checkout (payments are handled
  by Stripe). Nothing is charged during the {SERVICE.trialDays}-day trial.
- We email you 7 days before the trial ends. If you do nothing, your card is charged
  automatically and the schedule keeps working.
- Cancel any time on the Billing page. You keep access to the end of the period you paid
  for.

## Everything is included

- Weekly and monthly schedule, drag and drop, the same shift on several days at once
- Planning and locked weeks: employees only see what you publish
- Email invitations; employees log in with their own password
- "My shifts" on phones, installable like an app, readable offline
- English and Finnish, your own timezone and opening hours
- Export of all your data at any time

## Questions

**Several bars?** Each bar is one subscription item; adding a bar adds one more to your
bill, prorated.

**What if a payment fails?** Stripe tries again over a few days and we let you know. If
it still fails, the subscription ends: the schedule is then read-only for
{SERVICE.readOnlyDays} days, then locked, and the data is deleted
{SERVICE.deleteAfterDays} days after the end. Subscribing again brings everything back
until then.

**Can I take my data with me?** Yes. The owner can download everything as JSON, and the
shifts as a spreadsheet, at any time, also when the bar is locked.

**Would you rather run it yourself?** The same software is free and open source; see
[self-hosting](../self-hosting/install/).
