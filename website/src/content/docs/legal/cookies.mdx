## This website

The website sets **no cookies** and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## The app

The app only uses what it needs to work, so it doesn't ask for consent.

| Name | What it is for | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `session` (cookie) | Keeps you logged in. It can't be read by scripts on the page. | 7 days, or until you log out |
| `support` (cookie) | Only for the service's administrators, while they look at a bar in read-only support mode. | 1 hour |
| Offline copy (browser cache) | The app keeps the schedule you last loaded so you can see it without a connection. | Until you log out |

## Other services

- **Stripe** shows the checkout and billing pages. Stripe sets its own cookies there,
  for payments and fraud prevention; see [Stripe's cookie policy](https://stripe.com/cookie-settings).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that the sign-up form is filled in by a person. It
  doesn't use cookies to track you; see
  [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
