This policy explains how personal data is handled in the hosted myWorkSchedule service
and on this website, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

<Operator />

There are two roles, depending on the data:

- **The bar is the controller** of its work schedule data: its employees' names, email
  addresses, roles and shifts. We process that data only on the bar's behalf, under the
  [data processing agreement](../dpa/). If you're an employee, contact your bar first.
- **We are the controller** of the data we need to run the Service for our customers:
  the owner's account, billing, support requests and the security logs described below.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account: name, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash) | Logging in, invitations, password reset | Contract | While the account exists |
| Bar details: name, country, timezone, opening hours | Running the Service | Contract | While the bar exists |
| Billing: the owner's email, the subscription and its status. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see them | Charging for the Service, VAT | Contract; legal obligation (accounting) | Invoices 6 years (Finnish Accounting Act); the rest while the subscription lasts |
| Last time each user was active | Showing support who uses a bar; spotting problems | Legitimate interest | While the account exists |
| Audit log: support access, exports, deletions, subscription changes | Security and accountability | Legitimate interest | 2 years |
| Emails with us | Support | Contract; legitimate interest | 2 years after the matter closes |

We don't sell personal data, don't use it for advertising, and don't do automated
decision-making about people.

## Who else handles it

The companies that help us run the Service are listed on the
[subprocessors page](../subprocessors/). Some of them may process data outside the EU;
such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decisions or its standard
contractual clauses.

## How long

Data is kept as in the table above. When a bar's subscription ends, its data is deleted
{SERVICE.deleteAfterDays} days after the end; when an owner deletes a bar, it's deleted
after {SERVICE.deletionGraceDays} days. Backups are overwritten within 30 days after that.

## Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, to limit
its use, or object to it. Write to the address above. Bar owners can download their bar's
data and delete the bar themselves in the app. We answer within a month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection
Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en)).

## This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The server that hosts it
(GitHub Pages) records technical data such as IP addresses in its own logs, for security.
