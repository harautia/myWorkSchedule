This data processing agreement (DPA) is part of the [terms of service](../terms/). It
applies when the Customer (the **controller**) uses the hosted Service, and the operator
(the **processor**) processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, as required by
article 28 of the GDPR.

**Processor:**

<Operator />

## 1. What is processed

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject matter** | Providing the Service: planning, publishing and showing work shifts. |
| **Duration** | While the Customer's subscription lasts, and the deletion periods after it (section 7). |
| **Nature and purpose** | Storing, showing and transferring data so the Customer's staff can see and plan their shifts; sending invitations and notices by email; backups. |
| **People concerned** | The Customer's employees and managers. |
| **Kinds of data** | Name, email address, username, role, work shifts, login times, and what the Customer's users enter in the Service. No special categories of data. |

## 2. The processor's duties

The processor:

1. processes the data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are these
   terms and the Customer's use of the Service, unless the law requires otherwise;
2. ensures that the people processing the data are bound to confidentiality;
3. takes the security measures described in section 4;
4. uses subprocessors only as in section 5;
5. helps the Customer answer requests from the people concerned (access, correction,
   erasure), as far as possible through the Service's own tools: exporting the bar's
   data, anonymising an employee, and deleting the bar;
6. helps the Customer with security, breach notification, data protection impact
   assessments and prior consultation, taking into account the nature of the processing;
7. deletes or returns the data at the end, as in section 7;
8. gives the Customer the information needed to show that these duties are met, and
   allows audits by the Customer or an auditor it appoints, at the Customer's cost and
   with reasonable notice.

## 3. The Customer's duties

The Customer has a lawful basis for the data it puts in the Service, tells its staff about
the processing, and gives only lawful instructions.

## 4. Security

The data is protected with, among other things: encrypted connections (HTTPS); passwords
stored only as salted one-way hashes; strict separation of each bar's data; access by the
operator's staff only when needed for support, read-only, and recorded in an audit log;
daily backups; and keeping the software up to date. The security review is published in
the [source code repository](https://github.com/harautia/myWorkSchedule/blob/master/docs/security-review.md).

## 5. Subprocessors

The Customer gives general permission to use the subprocessors listed on the
[subprocessors page](../subprocessors/). The processor tells the Customer at least 30 days
before adding or replacing one; the Customer may object on reasonable grounds and, if no
solution is found, cancel the Service. The processor imposes the same data protection
duties on its subprocessors and remains responsible for them.

## 6. Personal data breaches

The processor tells the Customer without undue delay, and at the latest within 48 hours,
after becoming aware of a breach affecting the Customer's data, with the information the
Customer needs to notify the authority and the people concerned.

## 7. When the Service ends

When the subscription ends, the Customer can download its data until it's deleted:
{SERVICE.deleteAfterDays} days after the end, or {SERVICE.deletionGraceDays} days after the
Customer deletes the bar. Backups are overwritten within 30 days after that. Invoices are
kept as accounting law requires.

## 8. Transfers outside the EU

Data is stored in the EU. If a subprocessor processes it outside the EU or EEA, the
transfer relies on an adequacy decision or on the European Commission's standard
contractual clauses.
